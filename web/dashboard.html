Data Privacy Notice

In compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), this system collects the following data during a monitoring session:

  • Live video feed from the classroom camera
  • Body pose keypoints of the faculty member
  • Teaching activity classifications and timestamps
  • Session details (instructor, subject, section, room)

Purpose of Collection

Data is used solely for academic quality assurance monitoring. It is not used for disciplinary action without proper institutional review.

Data Retention

Session records are stored locally on this device only. No data is transmitted to external servers or third parties.

Rights of the Data Subject

The faculty member has the right to:

  • Be informed of the monitoring session
  • Access their session records upon request
  • Request correction of inaccurate records

Consent

By clicking I Agree and Start Recording, the authorized personnel confirms:

  1. The faculty member has been informed of this session.
  2. This recording is for academic purposes only.
  3. All data will be handled with confidentiality.
